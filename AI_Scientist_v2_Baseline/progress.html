Why the bigger model won

The depth sweep with everything else held fixed. Quality is single-peaked at 85.9M parameters while GPU utilisation climbs the whole way — so optimising utilisation directly would have picked the worst of the three. This is why a Chinchilla-style tokens-per-parameter argument fails here: under a wall-clock budget a larger model is handed more actual FLOPs, rather than spending a fixed number differently.

What each lever is worth — and why they don't add up

Grey is what the lever measured when it was first introduced. Green is what removing it from the finished configuration costs. Every lever is worth more in the final config, because a single-lever ablation measures a marginal contribution — what it is worth given everything else is present. Marginals cannot be summed: these five total 0.0363 against an actual improvement of 0.0213. The same gain is credited to several levers at once.

What has been established

  • The improvement generalises across seeds — and 14% of the headline was selection. Measuring the baseline and the final config at four seeds gives 2.16% / 1.72% / 1.89% / 1.95%, mean 1.93% ± 0.18. Seed 42 is the seed the search optimised on; held-out seeds average 1.85%. The gap is the winner's curse operating on the whole search, one level above the replicate case.
  • The reported best is a biased statistic. The incumbent's minimum (0.969615) is the luckiest of 16 byte-identical runs whose mean is 0.969890. The mean is the estimator; the minimum never moves upward, which is exactly what makes it biased.
  • The two largest effects are losses from replacing a primitive the baseline chose. relu² → GeLU costs +0.0254; removing QK normalisation costs +0.0147 — the latter despite producing the campaign's highest MFU (46.3%) and 4% more steps. Both are quality effects with throughput moving the wrong way.
  • Ablating the final config gives ~2× the introduction value. Removing a lever from the finished configuration costs more than adding it to an unfinished one: FINAL_LR_FRAC 0.00053 → 0.00142, shape 0.00423 → 0.00791, max-autotune 0.00125 → 0.00212. The levers compound.
  • Capacity is capped near 86M, confirmed five independent ways: depth 6/9/12, width 512/768, value-embedding coverage, MLP ratio 3×/5×, and retests of all three at the final operating point.
  • Six of nine optimizer knobs sit on the baseline's own default. Only settings that scale with step count moved — EMBEDDING_LR, UNEMBEDDING_LR, FINAL_LR_FRAC — plus WEIGHT_DECAY.
  • The noise floor is a property of the cluster, not the code. Byte-identical replicates gave sd 0.00013 early in the run and 0.00044 late, with step counts drifting 1316 → 1301 under load. All 134 trials ran on a shared box; 19 were refused as contended or OOM rather than scored.
