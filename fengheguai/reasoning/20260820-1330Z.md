## t0011 · explore · discard · val_bpb 0.978684

- **best after:** 0.978165

**Hypothesis (pre-registered).** This model needs far less long-range attention than the recipe provides. Two independent measurements point the same way: halving the short window from 1024 to 512 in t0002 gained 0.001882, and document masking in t0010 gained 0.002083 by forbidding attention outside the current document. Both say the useful support of attention is local. The SSSL pattern still spends two of eight layers at the full 2048 context, and those two layers cost 12.6M of the 44.1M attention FLOPs per token. Dropping to a single full-context layer, which the code already forces as the last layer, should convert that into roughly 4 percent more optimizer steps at little quality cost.

**Change.** One constant changed on champion t0010: WINDOW_PATTERN from SSSL to S. Because _compute_window_sizes cycles the pattern across layers and then unconditionally forces the final layer to the full window, this yields seven 512-token short layers plus one full-context layer, instead of six short and two full. Capacity, HEAD_DIM, dense value embeddings, document masking, batch 2**18, learning rates and every schedule are unchanged.

**Predicted effect.** Expected decrease of roughly 0.000-0.002 bpb versus champion t0010 at 0.978165, driven by about 4 percent more steps. This is a deliberately marginal bet: if a single global layer is insufficient to route document-level context, val_bpb will rise by a similar amount, which would mark the end of the attention-reduction direction that has paid off twice.

**Stated risk.** The direction has been pushed three times now and must saturate somewhere; one full-context layer may be too few for the model to aggregate information across a document, and unlike the earlier window reductions this removes a qualitatively distinct layer type rather than shrinking a span. The gain is also small enough that it must clear the 0.0001 repeat-noise level to be trusted, which the confirmation run will decide.

## t0012 · refine · keep · val_bpb 0.977934

- **best after:** 0.977934  ← new champion

**Hypothesis (pre-registered).** The t0004 gain was measured on a bundle of three learning rates moved together, and its own risk note recorded that a win would not attribute the gain to any one parameter group. That debt now matters because setup_optimizer puts the value embeddings in the same group as the token embeddings, and t0008 showed the value-embedding path is the most productive mechanism in this recipe, extending it to all eight layers and doubling those parameters to 33.5M. A parameter group that large and that load-bearing may not want the square-root batch rescale that helped the matrix and unembedding groups. Restoring EMBEDDING_LR alone to its original 0.6, while leaving UNEMBEDDING_LR and MATRIX_LR at their rescaled values, decomposes the bundle and tests whether the embedding group was rescaled correctly.

**Change.** One learning rate changed on champion t0010: EMBEDDING_LR 0.4243 back to 0.6, which drives both transformer.wte and all eight value embeddings. UNEMBEDDING_LR stays 0.002828 and MATRIX_LR stays 0.02828, so the square-root rescale remains in force for the other two groups. Batch 2**18, document masking, dense value embeddings, the SSSL pattern with the 512-token short window, capacity and every schedule are unchanged.

**Predicted effect.** Expected change between a 0.002 decrease and a 0.001 increase versus champion t0010 at 0.978165, and either sign is informative. A decrease means the embedding group was over-damped by the bundled rescale and that the enlarged value-embedding table wants a faster rate, which would suggest pushing it above 0.6 next. An increase confirms the square-root rescale was right for all three groups and closes the learning-rate axis for good.

**Stated risk.** A higher embedding rate can destabilise early training, since wte is bf16 and feeds a norm immediately; instability would show as a much higher loss or a FAIL exit rather than a small regression. This node also only decomposes one of the three groups, so if it regresses the unembedding group remains unattributed, though that group is 4.2M parameters against 37.7M here and is the least likely to matter.
