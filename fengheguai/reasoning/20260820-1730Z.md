## t0033 · explore · discard · val_bpb 0.976978

- **best after:** 0.973163

**Hypothesis (pre-registered).** The rotary frequency spectrum is tuned for a context this model never sees. RoPE with base 10000 spreads inverse frequencies so the slowest channels have wavelengths of order 60000 positions, which is appropriate when attention spans thousands of tokens. This model attends over 512 tokens in nine of its twelve layers and at most 2048 in the other three, and document masking cuts many spans shorter still. Across a 512-token window the low-frequency channels rotate by a tiny fraction of a cycle and are therefore nearly constant, so a large part of the head dimension carries almost no positional information. Two independent results in this campaign say the model genuinely wants local attention rather than merely tolerating it: narrowing the window to 512 gained 0.001882 in t0002, and widening it back to 1024 lost 0.001186 in t0030 while costing only 1.2 percent of steps, so it was a quality preference and not a throughput trade. Lowering the base to 1000 matches the spectrum to the span actually used and should give finer positional resolution where the model is looking, lowering val_bpb.

Both call sites, in __init__ and in init_weights, use the default, so the whole rotary table is rebuilt at the new base.
