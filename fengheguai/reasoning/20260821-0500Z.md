## t0092 · refine · discard · val_bpb 0.996374

- **best after:** 0.968389

**Hypothesis (pre-registered).** The initialisation fix in t0087 improved the value-embedding path but simultaneously made its update quantisation 32 times coarser, and nothing has corrected for that. These tables are stored as bf16 parameters, cast in init_weights alongside wte. bf16 carries 8 mantissa bits, so its resolution scales with magnitude: near the old element scale of 0.044 the ulp was 0.00024, but at the corrected scale near 1.0 it is 0.0078. AdamW writes its update directly into that bf16 tensor, so any per-row update smaller than the ulp is silently rounded away. This bites hardest exactly where it matters: embedding gradients are sparse, so a given row is touched only when its token appears and its Adam update is often far below the nominal rate, and the warmdown drives the effective rate to a tenth of peak over the final half of the budget. Keeping the tables in fp32 while casting the gathered rows to the activation dtype preserves those small updates at no change to the forward computation.

**Change.** Precision change on champion t0089: the value-embedding tables are no longer cast to bf16 in init_weights, so they remain fp32 parameters with fp32 AdamW moments, while GPT.forward casts each gathered row to x.dtype at the point of use so attention still receives bf16 and the fa3 kernel is unaffected. transformer.wte keeps its bf16 cast, since its elements sit near the scale where bf16 resolution is adequate and it is normalised immediately in forward. The corrected init of uniform plus or minus sqrt(3) and the decoupled VALUE_EMBEDDING_LR of 0.3 are retained; the 256-channel gate from t0091 is not, having cost 0.000509. Roughly 50M parameters move from bf16 to fp32, adding about 200MB of parameter and moment memory against the 35GB this configuration currently uses.

**Predicted effect.** Expected between a 0.001 decrease and a 0.0005 increase versus champion t0089 at 0.968389. The step count is the diagnostic to watch: the gather now reads twice the bytes and the cast is an extra elementwise pass, so if throughput falls more than a percent or two the quality gain could be offset. The forward computation is numerically identical up to the cast, so any difference beyond that is attributable to updates that previously did not survive rounding.

**Stated risk.** The dtype cast at the gather is exactly the failure that broke t0044, where a bf16 tensor multiplied by an fp32 parameter promoted the product and the fa3 kernel rejected it; here the cast is applied at the gather so ve is bf16 before it reaches the attention module, which keeps v + gate * ve in bf16 throughout. The quantitative case is also weaker than it first appears: nominal late-training updates are about 0.03, which is still four ulps, so only the sub-nominal per-row updates are being recovered rather than all of them, and the size of that population is not something this node can measure directly.

## t0093 · explore · failed · val_bpb —

- **best after:** 0.968389

**Hypothesis (pre-registered).** t0092 showed the value-embedding path is precision-limited but bought the fix in the most expensive place. Holding those tables in fp32 improved quality per step, loss 3.013 against the champion 3.029 at step 1000, yet throughput collapsed from 105ms to 211ms per step and the run lost 36 percent of its steps, finishing at 0.996374, worse than the original baseline. The cost came from tripling memory traffic on the largest parameter block, 50M parameters as fp32 weights plus fp32 moments. There is a cheaper place to spend that precision. _step_adamw allocates its moment buffers with torch.zeros_like(p), so a bf16 parameter silently receives bf16 moments, and the moments are where small updates must survive across steps: exp_avg holds a running average divided by the square root of exp_avg_sq, and both sit at an ulp of 0.0078 near the corrected embedding scale. Keeping parameters in bf16, so gathers and writes stay cheap, while allocating moments in fp32 should capture most of the quality effect at a fraction of the traffic.

**Change.** Optimizer change on champion t0089: AdamW moment buffers are allocated as float32 regardless of parameter dtype instead of inheriting it through zeros_like. The value embeddings and wte keep their bf16 casts in init_weights, so the forward pass, the embedding gathers and the parameter writes are unchanged from the champion. This affects the four AdamW groups, of which only the value embeddings are large enough to matter, adding roughly 200MB of moment memory against the 34GB this configuration uses. adamw_step_fused is compiled with dynamic=False and will recompile once for the new dtype mix, at a step-zero cost excluded from the timed budget. The fp32 parameter experiment from t0092 is not carried forward.

**Predicted effect.** Expected between a 0.001 decrease and a 0.001 increase versus champion t0089 at 0.968389. num_steps is the decisive diagnostic: the champion runs 2875 and t0092 ran 1828, so a count above roughly 2700 means the cheaper placement worked and the quality effect can be read directly, while a fall toward 2000 means moment precision carries most of the traffic cost as well and the direction closes.

**Stated risk.** The mixed-dtype update inside adamw_step_fused is the specific hazard: p is bf16 while exp_avg, exp_avg_sq and the derived denominator are fp32, so p.add_ downcasts on write and lerp_ mixes dtypes on read. That is legal in PyTorch and should not raise, but it is the same class of silent promotion that broke t0044, and if inductor materialises fp32 intermediates for the whole update the traffic saving will not appear. It is also possible the precision that mattered in t0092 lived in the parameter rather than the moments, in which case this measures a null and the honest conclusion is that the effect cannot be bought inside the step budget.

## t0094 · debug · discard · val_bpb 0.968509

- **best after:** 0.968389

**Hypothesis (pre-registered).** Unchanged from t0093: the value-embedding path is precision-limited, as t0092 demonstrated by improving loss per step from 3.029 to 3.013 at step 1000, and the cheap place to spend that precision is the AdamW moment buffers rather than the parameters, because _step_adamw allocates them with torch.zeros_like(p) and a bf16 parameter therefore receives bf16 moments with an ulp of 0.0078 near the corrected embedding scale. t0093 did not test this. It aborted during compilation of adamw_step_fused with a fake-tensor failure on exp_avg.lerp_(grad, 1 - beta1_t): the moment buffer is now fp32 while grad remains bf16, and an in-place operation cannot promote its own destination. The diagnosis is a dtype contract inside the fused step, not a defect in the idea.

**Change.** Debug repair of t0093 with the mechanism intact and the arithmetic unchanged. adamw_step_fused now states its dtypes explicitly for the mixed-precision case: the gradient is cast once to the moment dtype and reused for both lerp_ calls, and the parameter update folds the step size into the fp32 expression before a single cast back to the parameter dtype, since add_ likewise cannot write an fp32 result into a bf16 destination. For the fp32 parameter groups both casts are no-ops. The moment buffers stay float32 as in t0093, and the value embeddings and wte keep their bf16 casts, so gathers, the forward pass and parameter storage are exactly as in champion t0089.

**Predicted effect.** Expected between a 0.001 decrease and a 0.001 increase versus champion t0089 at 0.968389, with num_steps the decisive quantity. The champion runs 2875 steps and the fp32-parameter version in t0092 ran 1828; anything above roughly 2700 means the precision was successfully relocated to somewhere cheap and the quality effect can be read directly, while a fall toward 2000 means the moment buffers carry most of the traffic cost too and the direction closes.

**Stated risk.** Two casts now appear inside a fullgraph-compiled function, so inductor may materialise an extra fp32 temporary per parameter rather than fusing the cast into the existing kernel, which would reintroduce part of the traffic cost this node exists to avoid. The repair is also the third consecutive node on this thread, and the honest reading if it measures null is that the precision effect seen in t0092 cannot be separated from the cost of storing it, at which point the direction should be abandoned rather than relocated again.
