## t0103 · explore · discard · val_bpb 0.974490

- **best after:** 0.967286

**Hypothesis (pre-registered).** Averaging the endpoint of the trajectory paid 0.001103 in t0096, and the natural question is whether averaging the trajectory itself pays too. Lookahead does that: a slow copy of the weights follows the fast ones by a fraction alpha every k steps, and the fast weights are then reset onto the slow copy, so the optimizer explores from a point that is itself an average of recent iterates rather than from the latest one. It is a different intervention from the EMA, which only changes what is evaluated and never influences training, whereas this changes the path. The conditions here favour it: the run takes about 2870 steps at a batch of 2**17, which is the noisiest per-step gradient this campaign has used, and t0096 established that this model reaches its end as a noisy draw rather than a converged point. The cost is small and checkable, roughly 1GB of traffic every five steps against a 105ms step.

**Change.** Lookahead added to champion t0096, with LOOKAHEAD_K of 5 and LOOKAHEAD_ALPHA of 0.5. Every fifth optimizer step, an fp32 slow copy of every parameter is moved halfway toward the live weights and the live weights are copied back from it. The existing endpoint EMA is retained unchanged at EMA_START 0.7 and EMA_DECAY 0.99, so the two mechanisms coexist: Lookahead shapes the trajectory during training, the EMA denoises what is finally evaluated. Everything else matches the champion, including the linear anneal that t0102 confirmed beats cosine, WARMDOWN_RATIO 0.5, FINAL_LR_FRAC 0.1, the corrected value-embedding init, VALUE_EMBEDDING_LR 0.3, depth 12, the SSL pattern with a 256-token short span, document masking, output softcap 13, batch 2**17 and WEIGHT_DECAY 0.146.

**Predicted effect.** Expected between a 0.002 decrease and a 0.003 increase versus champion t0096 at 0.967286, with step count falling by perhaps one percent from 2870. The range is wide in both directions because this is the first change in many trials that alters the optimisation path rather than a coefficient in it, and because it interacts with two optimizers at once: the Muon groups carry their own momentum and NorMuon variance buffers, and resetting the parameters underneath those buffers every five steps is not something either was designed for.

**Stated risk.** That interaction is the main hazard and it is specific. Muon maintains a momentum buffer and a second-moment buffer keyed to the parameters it is updating, and Adam maintains exp_avg and exp_avg_sq likewise; Lookahead rewrites the parameters without touching any of that state, so for five steps after each sync the optimizers are correcting a trajectory that no longer exists. Standard Lookahead accepts this, but standard Lookahead is usually paired with plain SGD or Adam rather than with an orthogonalised update whose scale is set by a running variance estimate. If it regresses sharply, that mismatch is the likeliest cause rather than the averaging idea, and the informative follow-up would be a larger k rather than a different alpha.
