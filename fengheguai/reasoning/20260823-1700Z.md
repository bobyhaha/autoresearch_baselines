## t0353 · explore · keep · val_bpb 0.966202

- **best after:** 0.966202  ← new champion

**Hypothesis (pre-registered).** Every head in this champion must spend its full attention mass somewhere, because softmax normalises to one no matter what the window contains. That constraint bites hardest here: the SSL pattern gives two of every three layers a 256 token span, so most heads look at a short window that frequently holds nothing worth retrieving, and they are still forced to emit a convex combination of whatever is inside it. An attention sink is the usual escape, letting mass drain to a null slot, but this FA3 build exposes no sink argument and returns the softmax lse only as a non-differentiable auxiliary, so reconstructing a sink from the lse would silently produce wrong gradients rather than an error. The same escape is available downstream of the kernel: a per-head multiplicative gate on the attention output lets a head damp its own contribution toward zero when its window is uninformative, which is what draining mass to a sink accomplishes. If the forced-mass constraint is really costing this model, the gate should pay; if heads always have something useful inside a 256 token window, it should be neutral.

**Change.** One mechanism added to champion t0096. CausalSelfAttention gains attn_gate, an nn.Linear from the first 128 channels of x to n_head with no bias, constructed exactly like the existing ve_gate beside it. In forward, immediately before the heads are flattened for c_proj, the head-shaped attention output is multiplied by 2*sigmoid(attn_gate(x[..., :128])).unsqueeze(-1), the same 2*sigmoid form the value-embedding gate already uses, so the gate sits near one at initialisation and the change starts as near-identity. Placing it before the flatten covers the varlen and dense branches with a single edit. The new weight lives inside transformer.h, so setup_optimizer collects it into matrix_params, the parameter-count assertion still balances, and its (n_head, 128) shape joins the Muon shape-group that ve_gate already occupies. Nothing else moves: depth 12, SSL with a 256 short span, document masking, squared ReLU left unbounded after t0351 and t0352, softcap 13, EMA at 0.7 and 0.99.

**Predicted effect.** Expected between 0.966 and 0.968 against champion t0096 at 0.967286, with steps near 2850, since the gate adds one 128-by-4 matmul and a sigmoid per layer, well under a percent of a step dominated by the unembedding and MLP matmuls. A decrease would say the forced-mass constraint is real and short-window heads want an escape; a null at unchanged step count would say heads reliably find something useful inside 256 tokens and the constraint was not binding.

**Stated risk.** The gate is strictly more expressive than a sink, since it can scale a head output continuously rather than only shrink it toward zero, so a regression would be ambiguous between the mechanism being useless and the extra capacity being harmful. This campaign has repeatedly punished added control capacity: lookahead cost 0.0072 and 0.0024 in t0103 and t0104, and resid_lambdas at 0.95 cost 0.0043 in t0108, so the harmful-capacity branch is the likelier regression. If it regresses I will not scan the gate width or its initialisation; the informative follow-up is the strictly-shrinking form, a plain sigmoid bounded to zero-to-one rather than zero-to-two, which can only damp and so separates the sink hypothesis from the added-capacity confound.
