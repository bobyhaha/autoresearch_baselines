The interpretive risk I want to guard against: if this wins, the temptation is to read 'narrow the MLP' as a direction and keep going to 2x. Two points do not make a direction -- that is the extrapolation error this campaign has made three times -- which is why the 5x arm is queued behind this one regardless of what this returns.
