This is deliberately the configuration that timed out at t0483 -- the difference is that its training graph is now in the shared cache. The eval graph may still be cold, which is the specific way this can fail.

**Predicted effect.** Step count is the mechanism, as with every throughput bet. If the cache carries the compile, I expect ~2880 steps against the champion's 2806 (+2.7%), worth about -0.0016.
The registered failure mode is a third timeout. t0483 died in EVALUATION, and the eval path compiles its own graph -- which is precisely what defeated the depth-13 warm retries at t0438. If that happens here, the eval graph will then be cached too and a further attempt becomes cheap; if it happens twice more I stop, because at that point the cast and autotune are simply incompatible within this wall clock and t0484's standalone version is the honest ceiling.

**Stated risk.** Spending a slot on a configuration that has already timed out once, which I have done before and which worked twice (t0433, t0437) and failed twice (t0438, t0481). The difference between those cases was whether the EVAL graph got cached, not the training graph, and I do not know which side of that line this falls on.
If it wins, the champion would carry both a bf16 lm_head and max-autotune, and the second is already known to make every future structural bet expensive -- it is what forces the compile revert on graph-changing trials. Promoting this makes the campaign faster and further experiments harder, which is a real trade rather than a free win.
